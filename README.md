# LedgerAi - Restaurant & Wholesale Supply ERP

Multi-Tenant Enterprise Cloud ERP & AI Financial Munshi with live ledger calculations, real-time stock control, bilingual Tax Invoices, and automated Master Business Audit reports.

## Features
- **AI Financial Munshi & Voice Copilot:** Natural language and voice order booking, expense recording, and real-time ledger intelligence.
- **Bilingual Tax Invoices:** Official A4 receipts with Arabic/English letterheads, TRN, and QR compliance.
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture:** Complete data isolation per company.
- **PostgreSQL Cloud Sync:** Multi-device synchronization across laptops and mobile devices.
- **Automated Audit & P&L Reports:** Master Business Profit Audit, Stock Movement Ledger, and item/salesman level profit analysis.

## Development
